📐 CuraFrame v5.0.0 Architecture Overview

The CuraFrame Interaction Analysis Engine represents a fundamental shift towards quantitative, non-linear safety modeling. Instead of simple pass/fail flags, CuraFrame models therapeutic design spaces across five core layers:

📊 Safety Margins

Safety margins normalize distances to individual constraint boundaries. This enables medicinal chemists to identify how "safely" a compound compliant with core parameters resides inside the active boundaries.

🧱 Physical Bottlenecks

The physical weakest link is the specific ADMET parameter closest to boundary failure (or exhibiting the worst violation). It isolating the critical hurdle that restricts the candidate's clinical viability.

🔬 Epistemic Bottlenecks

Epistemic weakest links highlight constraint domains built on low literature confidence or limited expert provenance. This highlights where early-stage in vitro assay validation is most urgently required.

🤝 Coupled ADMET Risks

Analyzes synergistically compounding liabilities, including Lipophilicity-hERG Cardiotoxicity, Lipophilicity-CYP3A4 DDI, CNS MPO Balance, and Lattice-Grease Solubility risks.

🛠️ Synthesis Guidance

Converts logical evaluation outputs directly into organic chemistry directives. Recommends dynamic bioisosteric options and structural adjustments to streamline lead optimization.
