🧬 CuraFrame v5.0.0 Core Engineering Principles

The v5.0.0 release introduces a suite of advanced features focused on quantitative, multivariable chemical systems exploration. CuraFrame evaluates compounds against explicitly defined physiological design envelopes without generating hypothetical molecules or providing clinical guidelines. Our reasoning engine utilizes five foundational structures:

📊 API Safety Margin Evaluation

Normalizes compliance margins for each active parameter. Gives medicinal chemists quantitative insight into how comfortably a candidate resides within safety limits, replacing strict binary pass/fail logic with continuous scaling.

🧱 Physical Weakest Links

Isolates the single most restrictive safety bottleneck in a candidate design. This pinpoints which physical, in vitro, or pharmacokinetic parameter is closest to violating constraints or exhibits the most severe failure.

🔬 Epistemic Weakest Links

Tracks and reports confidence levels for every active constraint. Identifies parameters built on lower levels of literature-validated or expert provenance, highlighting exactly where focused in vitro assays are most needed.

⚖️ Coupled/Synergistic Risks

Models synergistic risks between coupled parameters, such as Lipophilicity-hERG Cardiotoxicity, Lipophilicity-CYP3A4 DDI, CNS BBB Permeability Balance, and Lattice-versus-Grease Solubility.

🧪 Structure-Modification Guidance

Converts weakest-link diagnostics into targeted organic chemistry recommendations. Provides validated strategies to alter molecular weight, decrease hERG blockade, optimize PSA, and improve dissolution without losing target potency.
